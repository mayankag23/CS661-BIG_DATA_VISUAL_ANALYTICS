📱 Short-Form Video Dominance

Platforms like Snapchat (80%) and Instagram (65%) lead in short-form video consumption, catering to younger audiences with quick, engaging content.

🎬 YouTube for Long Content

YouTube (85%) remains the primary platform for long-form content, while also showing significant live streaming (25%) and audio content (15%) engagement.

💬 WhatsApp's Versatility

WhatsApp shows balanced usage across images (60%), text (35%), and stories (45%), making it the most versatile communication platform.

✍ Twitter for Text

With 70% text-based content, Twitter remains the top platform for real-time news and discussions despite increasing multimedia features.
